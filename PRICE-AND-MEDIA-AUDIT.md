# Apex Computers price and media audit

Audit date: 6 September 2026

## Result

- All 76 catalogue products were checked against their supplied flyer, sales-listing artwork or directly supplied product details.
- Every displayed catalogue price and every variant price matches the amount printed in its supplied source.
- The website now labels these clearly as prices from the supplied listings and asks customers to confirm today's price.
- One media error was corrected: the HP EliteBook x360 1030 G7 gallery contained five HP EliteBook x360 1040 G6 photos. The 1030 G7 listing now contains only its seven matching product photos, plus its own main image and flyer. The five affected media titles, search text and product associations now identify only the 1040 G6.
- Shared artwork that is intentional was retained: the two ZBook Firefly 14 G7 configurations share one chassis render, the two 14-inch M1 Pro listings share one flyer, the Netac/Hiksemi SSD listings share one flyer, and all three Microsoft Surface configurations share the same eight supplied photos.

The source check used OCR across the assigned flyers followed by visual inspection of uncertain or multi-price artwork. “Match” means the website value is the same as the supplied listing; it is not a promise that the item is still in stock at that price.

## Current-market spot check

Public Tanzania listings are useful only as a benchmark because used/refurbished condition, RAM, storage, warranty and seller terms differ. The supplied price therefore remains the business price on the website until Apex Computers changes it.

- Dell Latitude 3189 4GB/128GB: a MyKariakoo listing showed TSh 325,000, below the supplied TSh 460,000. Confirm the condition and final selling price before launch: <https://mykariakoo.co.tz/products/dell-latitude-3189>
- HP ProBook x360 11 G5 4GB/128GB: a Mchongotz listing showed TSh 450,000 with warranty terms, compared with the supplied TSh 470,000: <https://mchongotz.co.tz/products/hp-probook-x360-11-g5-ee-touchscreen-laptop/>
- HP EliteBook 840 G5 8GB/256GB: a public retailer listing showed TSh 680,000; the supplied listing is TSh 770,000: <https://kariakoomall.co.tz/product/hp-elitebook-840-g5-intel-core-i5-8th-gen-8gb-256gb-ssd-14-inch/>
- HP ProBook 430 G8 8GB/256GB: current public marketplace results included approximately TSh 550,000–650,000 for similarly labelled listings, compared with the supplied TSh 750,000: <https://jiji.co.tz/computers-and-laptops/hp-probook-430>

Owner action before public launch: confirm whether the two compact education models (Dell 3189 and HP ProBook x360 11 G5) include better condition, accessories, warranty or service that explains the higher supplied prices. Update only when the actual inventory and business decision are known.
